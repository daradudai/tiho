What Tiho does

Three quiet jobs, on every new message.

Hides the ugly

Insults, scam links, bots and copypasta floods are hidden or folded into a thin “2 hidden · show” line. One click brings them back.

Questions stand out

Real questions get a soft highlight, so the one person asking something doesn't drown in emotes.

Folds repeats

When 37 people type KEKW, you see one line: KEKW ×37.

Three levels

Pick one in the small bar above chat. Change it any time.

Off

Shows every message.

Default Clean

Hides insults, scams and spam. Questions stand out.

Quiet

Also hides one-word replies like “W” and “LOL”. Complaints are dimmed.

The streamer, mods and VIPs are never hidden. Streamers and mods can switch to Advanced mode for categories, stats and rules.

Where it stands

Plainly, so you know what you're getting.

  • It's early. We're testing it on real streams right now, and it still makes mistakes.
  • Nothing is lost. Anything Tiho hides is one click away, and you can tell it “not spam”.
  • Languages. So far we've checked it properly on English and about a dozen others. The rest is untested.
  • Your computer does the work. A small AI model inside the extension reads each new message. On older computers it can fall behind in very busy chats.
  • Free, no account. There's no Tiho server, so there's nothing to sign up for and nothing we could collect.

Nothing leaves your computer

Every message is checked inside your browser. No account, no analytics, no Tiho server. This website sets no cookies either.
